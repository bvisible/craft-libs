# Neoffice fork markers

Map of **our** divergence from upstream (`storytold/craft-libs`) in this fork (`bvisible/craft-libs`,
branch `version-15`). Everything that can carry a comment carries a `//// Neoffice — …` marker in
place: `// //// Neoffice — …` in Rust (a bare `////` is an error for clippy), `# //// Neoffice — …`
in TOML and YAML, `<!-- //// Neoffice — … -->` in Markdown. This file holds what cannot.

At the next upstream merge, `grep -rn "////"` in the source plus this file give the complete
picture of what is ours and why.

## Base of the divergence

Last upstream merge: `storytold/craft-libs` `main` at `ea1fbeb` (2026-11-02, 410 commits after the previous base
`7895f5e` of 2026-10-24). `git merge-base HEAD upstream/main` gives the base the marker check (CI "Neoffice fork
markers") compares against. That merge changed none of upstream's code. It moved the workspace to egui 0.37 (on
upstream's own `[patch]` of the storytold egui fork), so our two crates changed in three places only: the `egui_kittest`
dev-dependency of `craft-nora-ui` (`0.36` to `0.37`), a renamed accessibility role in one of its tests
(`WidgetType::Button` to `Role::Button`), and the comments that said the workspace was on 0.36.

## What the fork adds

Two crates, shared by every Neoffice editor (decision of 2026-10-13): `craft-nora`, the client core
of Nora, the Neoffice assistant (sans I/O: protocol, conversation state machine, document actions),
and `craft-nora-ui`, its egui panel. No upstream code was changed, apart from the two lines that
register them in the root `Cargo.toml` and the two lines that list them in `README.md`. They use
nothing else of this workspace, except upstream's `craft-ai-core`, which `craft-nora` reads behind its
`ai-core` feature (Nora's errors and turns in the vocabulary the Craft apps share for AI jobs, 17.10).

## 2026-11-05: the pane takes the design system's look, dictation, and a faster read

`craft-nora-ui` draws the Neoffice design system (palette light and dark, Karla and Forum, Lucide icons, Nora's sphere):
`look.rs`, `icons.rs`, `orb.rs`, `orb_rings.rs` (generated by `tools/gen_orb.py`), `fonts/`, and a microphone (`voice.rs`).
`craft-nora` learns what Nora is doing (`Activity`), turns a recording into words (`Input::Transcribe`, `Dictation`,
`protocol::STT_TRANSCRIBE`) and reads its replies and actions often right after something happened in the turn, not only in
its first fifteen seconds. The panel now needs egui 0.37 (it names widgets by their accesskit `Role`). Upstream's files touched:
`ATTRIBUTION.md` (rows for the fonts, the icons and the sphere) and `Cargo.lock` (the browser crates of the microphone).
